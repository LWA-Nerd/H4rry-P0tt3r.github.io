  • Use Some Notable AI

As aforementioned, AI has been integrated into many parts of our lives and continues to grow in usage. However, this does not mean AI is used in ways it was intended. There is no denying that artificial intelligence has been used with malicious intent, with technology such as deepfakes and essay-writing AIs. But with this, people must realize that this proves AI is impressive and is an outstanding feat of society. The potentially horrible things that AI has done is not a reflection of artificial intelligence, but of the user. The AI has been trained to answer problems to the best of its capabilities, not to determine the ethicality of answering it.
