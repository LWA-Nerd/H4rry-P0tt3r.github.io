The idea of AI was first percieved in the 1950s. It started with a man named Alan Turing who proposed something called the Turing Test to evaluate a machine's ability to emulate human intelligence. To put simply, the Turing Test is a way to measure how human-like an AI is. The first AI was programmed 2 years later by a man named Arthur Samuel. It learned to play checkers and built on its skill the more it played, just like a human, it would train itself by playing more games.



There have been many people since then who have been pivotal in the research and development of these AI models, becoming more and more complex as they do so. Today, artificial intelligence has become a great part of our lives and continues to get better with time. With a mere click on your phone, you can be met with AI chatbots ready to assist in everyday activities. Google has many uses of AI throughout the entirety of the company. Not only do they have chatbots such as Gemini and the Google Assistant on Google Pixel phones, they also have a Generative AI which compiles information from various sources to give the most accurate answer based on your Google search.
